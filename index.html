Overview

What is Tangentgarden?

A small painted woodland farm that keeps you company on your Windows desktop. The keys you press anyway become crops, materials and long-term projects.

Your keyboard is the farm. Each key has its own plot, and every real press moves that plot along: clearing, sowing, watering, growing and harvesting. Writing an email, coding or playing another game all count, because the game looks at which keys are pressed rather than what you write.

Harvests fill your storage. From there you choose what happens next: sell for gold, fill deliveries, feed your animal friends, or use the produce in recipes and building projects. Gold unlocks new seeds, friends, homes and decorations.

Work mode keeps the farm in a small transparent strip at the bottom of your screen, and clicks pass through to the programs behind it. When you have time, the full Meadow and the keyboard garden give you room for everything else.

You decide how much to do. There are no ads, no energy bars and no daily login. Your farm is saved on your own computer, and an optional account only adds shared statistics and a friends list.

How it plays

The core gameplay loop

Key presses do the growing. Every decision about what to do with the harvest is yours.

  1. 1

    Choose seeds

    Start with carrots, unlock new varieties in the Market, and pick what to grow. Unlocked seed packets can be used again and again.

  2. 2

    Use your keyboard

    Real key presses take each plot through clearing, sowing, watering and growing, even while you work in another program.

  3. 3

    Harvest

    Ripe crops go into your storage. Each variety has its own price, growing time and harvest size.

  4. 4

    Sell, deliver or feed

    Sell for gold, fill deliveries, feed your friends or save produce for recipes and projects.

  5. 5

    Unlock more

    Spend gold on new seeds, friends, building permits and decorations.

  6. 6

    Build and decorate

    Gather wood, stone and iron, deliver them to your building projects and arrange the Meadow as you like.

  7. 7

    Chase long-term goals

    Breed legendary crops, grow the World Tree and take on the optional endgame goals when you feel ready.

  8. ↻

    Back to work

    Return to whatever you were doing. The next harvest grows from the keys you press along the way.

Economy

Market, storage & deliveries

The Market turns harvests into gold, and deliveries give you something to aim for if you want a goal for the day.

The Market sells new seed varieties for gold. Each seed packet you unlock stays yours, and changing what you will plant next never replaces a plant that is already growing.

Storage holds your harvest, and you can protect varieties or keep a minimum amount so nothing you need gets sold by mistake. Crops come in normal, fine and perfect quality.

Deliveries include daily baskets that pay extra gold, and you can reserve stock for them. Missing a basket carries no penalty. Neighbour deliveries and an optional 28-contract campaign offer longer goals, with no deadlines.

Farm life

Animal friends

Twenty-two woodland friends can move in, each with their own name, favourite food and home.

A home for your friends

Invite and care. Invite a friend with their favourite food, then feed or pet them up to five times a day. If you put a friend out of view, they keep their friendship and stay yours.

Homes and rooms. Once a friend has been invited, you can buy a permit for their house and deliver the materials to build it. Clover, Moss and Pip also have rooms you can furnish.

Helpers. An invited friend can take over a chore, such as harvesting and replanting a chosen tree or working the mine. Helpers run on your ordinary key presses, not on time away, and they need five times as many presses as doing it by hand.

Stories and projects. Friends have their own stories, relationships and reactions, and they can work together on larger shared builds such as the greenhouse, the creek bridge and the picnic spot. The memory book keeps a record of real moments from your farm.

Your canvas

Build & decorate

Everything you earn can become part of a place that looks the way you want it to.

Building projects start with a permit and finish when the materials are delivered. Plant trees in the woodland grove for wood, and work the old mine for stone and iron. The project planner shows what a build still needs, but leaves the doing to you.

Crafting turns harvests and textiles into decorations you can place. Three theme collections reward you with a matching decoration once you have gathered each set.

Edit mode lets you move, resize, flip and layer objects, work with groups and undo your changes. You can save up to eight arrangements and swap between them without touching your money or progress, or share a layout as a blueprint.

Mood is yours to set as well. Choose day, dusk, night or your computer's clock for the light, pick a season, and enjoy the purely cosmetic weather. Photo mode freezes the scene and saves the Meadow as a transparent PNG.

Discovery

The garden journal

An animated book that gathers your crops, animals, goals and collections in one place.

Crop journal Herbarium collection

Eight chapters cover Crops, Animals, Goals, Orders, Decor, Focus, Fish and Endgame. Turn the pages as you would in a real book, or hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom.

Collections give you things to complete at your own pace: 12 pond fish, pressed plants in the herbarium, three theme collections of decorations and a list of milestones to earn along the way.

Focus offers a quiet work timer and a local task list. You start each session or break yourself, and time spent away from the game is not counted.

Fishing and expeditions add small side activities. Once your fishing corner is set up, everyday left clicks bring in a catch, and expedition maps let you send a friend off to discover new places and bring home finds.

Optional extra

The local typing trial

Farming never depends on how fast you type. If you enjoy testing yourself, the typing trial is a separate challenge you can open whenever you like.

Five practice tests run for two minutes each, from 20 WPM and 90% accuracy in the first test upwards. The trial measures only what you enter in its own typing area, and results are saved locally.

The final test combines all five sections in one attempt. Reaching at least 60 WPM and 98% accuracy in every section completes the Harvest Virtuoso goal.

The trial is optional. It has no farming requirements or economic rewards, and you never need it to unlock crops or other content.

Good to know: the game itself is in English, but the practice texts are deliberately in Swedish, including å, ä and ö.

Saves & sharing

Saves, statistics & the optional account

Your farm lives on your own computer. The account is only for sharing statistics and comparing with friends.

After your session

Local saves. Everything you need to play is saved on your computer. The game shows key presses per key for today and for all time, and a session summary shows what has become ready since you last looked.

Moving to another computer. Use Move whole farm to export your farm to a file and import it on the other machine. This is the way to move a farm, because accounts do not store or sync it.

The optional account. With an email address and password you can upload statistics and add friends using a friend code. Accepted friends appear in a list sorted by gold, so you can see how everyone's farm is doing.

🛡️

An account is not needed to download or play. Uploads contain statistics only, never your farm, what you type or which keys you pressed. See Privacy & trust for the full list.

Privacy & trust

Tangentgarden reads key states across your whole computer because that is how the farm grows. That understandably raises a question — "is this a keylogger?" — so here's a direct, plain-language answer.

🛡️

Tangentgarden is not a keylogger.

Key states grow your garden. Your typed text is not recorded. While running, Tangentgarden can detect key presses even when another app has focus. It uses them for gameplay and keeps counters, not a transcript of what you write.

🖥️

Works alongside other apps

Windows key and mouse button states are sampled while the game runs, without needing keyboard focus. Clicks anywhere on your computer are counted too, and left clicks can drive fishing.

🚫

No typed-text history

The ordinary input path checks which keys are down. It does not turn those samples into sentences, read documents, or keep an ordered log of your typing.

🔍

Inspect the input code

See real source excerpts below, with complete input files to read. The distinction is visible in the implementation: temporary key states and counters, rather than text recording.

🔑

Pause when you need to

Pause stops gameplay registration, and you can list programs that pause it automatically. For that list, the game compares the name of the program in front, not its window titles. These controls do not stop the sampling; exit the game to stop it.

🔒

Local counters & saves

Your farm saves include daily and lifetime counts per key. These are totals, not a sequence of presses. The separate typing trial uses text entered into its own practice field.

🎮

Account optional

Play offline without signing in. If you log in and choose to upload, your player name, gold, harvested crops, animal count and total key presses, mouse clicks and today's presses are sent about once a minute until you log out or close the game.

01

A key state, not a character

The high bit answers one question: is this virtual key down right now? This function returns a boolean, not typed text. It works across apps, so focus is not a privacy boundary.

C# · Main.csLines 160–164
private static bool IsDown(int key)
{
    // Den högsta biten visar om knappen är nere just nu.
    return (GetAsyncKeyState(key) & 0x8000) != 0;
}
02

A temporary, bounded queue

The worker samples roughly every 10 ms. The main thread takes samples out of the queue for gameplay. At 256 pending samples the queue is cleared, limiting the temporary backlog; it is not a saved typing history.

Totals per key, saved locally

These arrays count presses per key for today and all time. The local save preserves these totals. They contain neither the order of presses nor the words you typed.

These excerpts explain the ordinary input path; they are not an independent security audit or proof that a downloaded executable matches this source. The linked files are a dated source snapshot, not the entire game.

In technical terms: the Windows input path polls GetAsyncKeyState on a worker thread, approximately every 10 ms, without installing a keyboard hook. Short-lived samples contain key and mouse button states, cursor position, elapsed time and an app-blocking flag. The main thread consumes them for gameplay. Local saves retain progression and counters, not an ordered keystroke history.

The optional account is hosted on Supabase, which stores your email address, login details, friend connections and uploaded statistics. All account traffic uses HTTPS, and login passwords and session tokens are not saved to disk, so you log in again after restarting. Accepted friends can see your player name, gold, harvested crops, animal count, today's presses and when you last uploaded. Without an account the game makes none of these connections, and you can play with the network cable unplugged.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions you are most likely to have, especially about trust and what the game does with your keyboard.

Is Tangentgarden a keylogger?

Tangentgarden samples key states across apps for gameplay, but does not keep a transcript or an ordered keystroke log. Local saves include per-key counters. Inspect the input code above for the implementation and its limits.

Does the game read what I type in other apps?

It detects key presses even while other apps have focus, so your farm can grow while you work. The ordinary input path samples Windows key states rather than reading the text inside those apps. Pause or the program list stop gameplay registration; exit the game to stop sampling.

Why does the game need keyboard input at all?

Because ordinary key presses are what grow your crops. Each press advances the plot belonging to that key, including when you are working in another program.

Do I have to type fast or practice?

No. Farming counts key presses, not speed or accuracy, and there are no words to type. The local typing trial is a separate, optional challenge with no effect on what you can grow.

Does my farm grow when the game is closed?

No. Crops and materials grow from key presses made while the game is running. A few things do follow real time, such as expeditions and how long food lasts.

Can my crops wither if I don't play?

By default, inactivity can bring moss and let plants slip back, and food can expire. If you would rather avoid that, turn on Keep the farm as I left it in Quiet settings. Plants still grow with your key presses and friends can still eat.

Does it collect passwords or personal information?

The ordinary input path does not reconstruct or save passwords or typed sentences. The optional account does ask for an email and password to authenticate with the account service over HTTPS. Login passwords and session tokens are not saved to disk in this version. Play offline without an account if you prefer.

Do I need an account to play?

No. The game is fully playable offline with no account. An optional account uploads selected statistics and enables the friends list. It does not store your farm or raw key samples.

Can I play on multiple computers?

You can move your farm with Move whole farm, which exports it to a file you import on the other computer. There is no automatic sync, and an account does not restore your farm.

Is the game really free?

Yes. Tangentgarden is free to play, with no ads, no microtransactions and no energy systems.

What platforms does it support?

Windows PC (64-bit) only. The way the game reads input and its desktop strip are both built on Windows features.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Only the optional account features use the network: logging in, uploading statistics, the friends list and password recovery.

Is there controller or mouse support?

You play with a keyboard and mouse. There is no controller support. The mouse is used for menus, arranging the farm and the journal, and everyday clicks also count towards activities such as fishing.

How long is the game?

There is no fixed end. The farm can keep growing for as long as you like, and the eight optional endgame goals are designed as long-term projects.

What are the system requirements?

The Download section lists what is known so far. You need 64-bit Windows and about 300 MB of free space.

Where can I download it?

A public download is not available yet. When it is, you will find it in the Download section on this page. The game comes as a program file with a data folder, not an installer, and the two need to stay together.
